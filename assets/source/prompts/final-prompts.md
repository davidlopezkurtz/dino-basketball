## Court Background, Landscape

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: mobile canvas game background, landscape court
Primary request: create a polished 2D cartoon outdoor dinosaur basketball court background for a preschool-friendly mobile browser game.
Scene/backdrop: bright blue sky, soft clouds, warm sun, playful distant rounded hills, colorful court and grass near the bottom.
Style/medium: high-quality playful 2D game illustration, rounded shapes, crisp clean edges, soft cel shading, cheerful sports-toy feeling.
Composition/framing: 16:9 landscape composition, no characters, no dinosaur, no ball, no hoop, no scoreboard, no text. Keep the lower 30 percent usable as the court/ground area, and keep the upper area open enough for gameplay objects.
Color palette: bright sky blue, fresh greens, warm court colors, small colorful accents; avoid dark moody colors and avoid a one-color palette.
Constraints: no text, no logos, no watermark, no UI, no basketball hoop, no players, no ball. Must feel like a finished game background, not a sketch.
