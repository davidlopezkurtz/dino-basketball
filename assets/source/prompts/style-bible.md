# Dino Basketball Visual Style Bible

Use a playful 2D preschool sports-cartoon style with rounded silhouettes, clean edges, soft cel shading, and high readability on phone screens.

Visual priorities:

- Bright outdoor court, clear sky, cheerful plants and rounded dinosaur-era hills.
- Friendly green dino character with simple expressions and readable poses.
- Orange and white basketball props with soft highlights and minimal texture noise.
- No text, logos, watermarks, brand marks, or UI labels inside generated assets.
- Transparent sprites use chroma-key source images and local background removal.

Runtime constraints:

- Backgrounds are WebP.
- Transparent props and characters are PNG.
- Keep first-load assets below 2.5 MB.
- Keep procedural drawing fallbacks in the game for missing or failed assets.
